Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any fees to join the platform?

We operate on a lightweight commission model. You only pay a small percentage on successful sales, with no upfront costs or monthly fees.

How is food safety ensured?

All products must be within their expiration date. We regularly audit product listings to ensure they meet safety standards.

How are refunds and complaints handled?

We provide a streamlined refund process and customer service support to help you efficiently resolve customer issues.

What types of products can I sell?

You can sell various food items including but not limited to produce, baked goods, dairy products, meats, and prepared foods, as long as they are within their expiration date and meet quality standards.
