Food Organic

Food organic waste includes edible and inedible parts of food from households or businesses. These materials are biodegradable and suitable for composting.

✅ Accepted Items:

  • ✔️ Fruit and vegetables
  • ✔️ Bread, rice, and grains
  • ✔️ Meat and fish (raw or cooked)
  • ✔️ Egg shells and coffee grounds

❌ Not Accepted:

  • ❌ Plastic packaging
  • ❌ Liquids (e.g., oil, soup)
  • ❌ Pet waste
  • ❌ Non-organic trash (metal, glass, etc.)
